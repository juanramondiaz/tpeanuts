    \small
    This document is the companion reference for the \texttt{core.BSM.bsm\_sterile} and
    \texttt{core.BSM.bsm\_mass\_spectrum} packages and every downstream consumer of a 4-flavour
    \pyclass{OscillationParameters.pmns}: the exact-Hamiltonian builders in
    \texttt{core.common.hamiltonian}, the closed-form Ferrari eigenvalue solver in
    \texttt{core.perturbative.spectral}, the numerical and perturbative evolutors, and the
    solar/Earth/atmosphere propagation pipelines. Chapter~1 covers the theoretical foundations of
    the 3+1 scheme: the short-baseline anomalies motivating it, the $4\times4$ mixing
    parametrisation, the sterile neutral-current term, and the exact SM-limit embedding.
    Chapter~2 is an exhaustive account of the \texttt{tpeanuts} implementation, including the
    closed-form quartic (Ferrari) eigenvalue solution specific to $N=4$. Chapter~3 documents the
    named sterile presets and the observables/simulations that can be built with them. Chapter~4
    walks through the validation notebooks that exercise the sterile extension and reports their
    actual results. Blue boxes (\textbf{Theoretical Foundation}) contain the physics; green boxes
    (\textbf{Implementation}) link that physics to actual \texttt{tpeanuts} code; orange boxes
    collect approximations and limitations; indigo boxes (\textbf{Result}) report a concrete
    numerical outcome of a notebook or of this project's own test suite. Text highlighted in
    \keyresult{yellow} marks key results/equations.
  \end{tcolorbox}
