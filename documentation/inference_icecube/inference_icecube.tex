    end to end, the real IceCube DeepCore 9-year "golden event" atmospheric-neutrino public data

    \texttt{tpeanuts} (unlike every other detector in this project, which folds a continuous
    differential cross section), the real detector-systematics hypersurface correction, the
    gradient-based Poisson fit, and the resulting real $\theta_{23}$/$\Delta m^2_{3l}$

    audit of the fitted muon-background normalization scale against IceCube's own published
    value. Blue boxes (\textbf{Theoretical Foundation}) contain the physics; green boxes
